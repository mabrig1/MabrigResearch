About Mabrig Research Institute

Mabrig Research Institute (MRI) is a Nigeria-based academic publishing organisation dedicated to advancing research output from African scholars, postgraduate students, and institutions.

We provide a full-stack publishing ecosystem — from manuscript submission and peer review to DOI assignment, citation tracking, and grant access through our partner platform AfriGrantPipeline.

Our flagship outlet, the Mabrig Journal of Interdisciplinary Research (MJIR), publishes original research across disciplines including public health, education, agriculture, social sciences, and technology.
